Flunkyball rules

Flunkyball is an outdoor drinking game. Two teams face each other with a bottle in the middle. Knock the bottle over with a ball? Then your team gets to drink until the other team has put the bottle back up. The first team to finish all their beers wins.

You will see it written as flunkyball, flunky ball or flunkybal. It is the same game.

The game is simple, but every park plays it with different rules. Below you will find the basic rules and the house rules to agree on first.

Setting up

You play Flunkyball on a field, in the park or at a campsite. You need about 10 metres of space.

  1. Make two teams of 3 to 8 players. Both teams are the same size.
  2. Mark two lines about 10 metres apart. Use a rope, flip-flops or bags.
  3. Fill a 1.5 litre plastic bottle a quarter to half full with water. Put it right in the middle.
  4. Every player puts a bottle or can of beer on the line in front of them. Everyone has the same beer and it stays closed for now.
  5. Get a ball ready. A tennis ball or a small football works best.

How to play

  1. The team with the shortest player starts.
  2. One player throws the ball at the bottle. Players on a team take turns throwing, without skipping anyone.
  3. Miss? Then the ball goes to the other team. They throw from their own line.
  4. Does the bottle fall over? Then the throwing team can open their beers and drink.
  5. Meanwhile the other team runs to the middle. They put the bottle back up and bring the ball back behind their own line.
  6. Is everyone back behind the line? Then they shout "stop". The throwing team puts their beers down right away.
  7. Then the other team throws. This goes on until someone has finished their beer.

Your beer stays closed until your team hits the bottle for the first time. Only then can you open it.

When is your beer finished?

Think your beer is empty? Then hold it upside down above your head. More than a sip comes out? Then you get a penalty beer.

Is your beer really empty? Then you go and stand on the sideline. You can't throw or run anymore. You can cheer your team on.

The first team to finish all their beers wins.

Penalties

For these mistakes you get a penalty beer, or the other team gets a sip. Agree beforehand which penalty you use.

  • You drink before the bottle has fallen over.
  • You keep drinking after "stop".
  • You cross the line before the ball has been thrown.
  • Your beer falls over, for whatever reason.
  • You pick up your beer while you are not drinking, for example to protect it from the ball.

House rules

There are no official Flunkyball rules. These are the differences you will see most.

  • The throw: some groups let you throw any way you like. Others want an overarm throw.
  • The distance: 10 metres is the most common. Too much space or too little? Adjust it.
  • Helping out: some groups let teammates drink each other's beer. Others call that cheating.
  • Hitting an opponent's beer: hit someone else's beer with the ball? Then they have to open a new one.
  • Without alcohol: the game works with soft drinks or alcohol-free beer too. As long as everyone has the same.

Tips to win

  • Don't let your best thrower go first. Spread your good throwers over the turn.
  • Put your fastest runners up front. The faster the bottle is back up, the less the others drink.
  • Drink in small, quick sips. You need to be able to stop at any moment.
  • Good at adten, downing a drink in one go? Then you have a big advantage here.

Where does Flunkyball come from?

Flunkyball comes from Germany and Poland. It was already played at festivals and universities there in the early 2000s. In Poland it is called flanki. It is almost a national student game there. Germany even has a yearly championship.

In the Netherlands and Belgium you mostly see it at student associations, at festivals and in the park when the weather is nice.
