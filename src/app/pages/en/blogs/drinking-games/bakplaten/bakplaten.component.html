Bakplaten

Bakplaten is a super simple Dutch card game. You draw a card. Is it a face card? Then it's your turn to drink. The name says it all: a "bak" means downing your drink and "platen" are the face cards.

How to play

  1. Take the jokers out of the deck.
  2. Shuffle the deck well.
  3. Put the deck face down on the table.
  4. Every player takes turns drawing a card.
  5. Turn the card over and put it on the discard pile.
  6. Drew a face card or an ace? Down your drink.
  7. Keep going until the deck is empty or until you are done.

When do you drink?

  • Drew a jack, queen, king or ace? Then you down your drink.
  • Drew a normal card? Then nothing happens.
  • Drew two cards by accident? Take an extra sip.

Extra rules

  • Want to make it heavier? Red cards mean you hand out, black cards mean you drink yourself.
  • Want to make it lighter? Don't count aces as face cards.
