Mijnenveld

Mijnenveld is a Dutch card game where you pick a path through the cards. Mijnenveld means minefield. Step on a face card or an ace and you are hit. Then you have to start over.

How to play

  1. Pick a dealer.
  2. The dealer lays the cards face down in rows of 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2 and 1 card.
  3. The player starts at the first card.
  4. The dealer turns over the chosen card.
  5. Is it a normal number card? Then the player can move on.
  6. From the next row on, the player picks left or right.
  7. The player tries to make it to the other side of the field this way.
  8. Does the player land on a jack, queen, king, ace or joker? Then it's a mine.
  9. Hit a mine? The player drinks and starts again at the front.
  10. The dealer replaces all the cards the player walked over.
  11. Is the pile of replacement cards empty? Then the penalty goes up by 1 sip.
  12. The game stops as soon as someone makes it through the field in one piece.

When do you drink?

  • You drink when you land on a mine.
  • With more players, everyone can drink when someone steps on a mine.

Extra rules

  • Play with fewer rows if you want a short round.
  • Make clear beforehand whether jokers are in. With jokers the game gets a lot meaner.
