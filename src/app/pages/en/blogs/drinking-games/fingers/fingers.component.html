Fingers

Fingers is a quick guessing game with fingers on a glass. You guess how many fingers stay on the rim and try to get out of the game that way. The Dutch call it Vingeren.

How to play

  1. Put a glass with a drink in it in the middle.
  2. Every player puts 1 finger on the rim of the glass.
  3. The first player counts down.
  4. During the countdown every player decides whether their finger stays or goes.
  5. Right after the countdown, the player whose turn it is calls out a number.
  6. That number is their guess for how many fingers stay on the glass.
  7. Count right away how many fingers are actually still on it.
  8. Was the player right? Then that player is safe and out of the game.
  9. Was the player wrong? Then they stay in the game.
  10. Then it's the next player's turn.
  11. Keep playing until 1 player is left.

When do you drink?

  • The last player left empties the glass.
  • Call out an impossible number and you lose right away.
  • Take your finger away too late and it counts as a finger on the glass.

Extra rules

  • In the final you have to win 2 times in a row.
  • Agree that nobody can let their finger hover. It's on or off.
