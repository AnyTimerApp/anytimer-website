Angry Mug

Angry Mug is an exciting game of luck on your phone. You tap happy mugs. But one of those happy mugs is actually the angry mug.

How to play

  1. Open Angry Mug in the AnyTimerApp.
  2. Pick the size of the board: 4, 9, 16, 25 or 36 mugs.
  3. Take turns tapping a happy mug.
  4. Is it a happy mug? Then it's the next player's turn.
  5. Tapped the angry mug? Then you lose.

Did you know you can play Angry Mug in our free app?

When do you lose?

  • Tapped the angry mug? Then you lose.
  • Tapped a happy mug? Then you are safe and the next player goes.

Pick the size

The size also decides how long the game takes. With 4 mugs the game is over fast. With 36 mugs it takes longer and gets more and more exciting.
