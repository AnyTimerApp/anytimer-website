Imposter game rules

Imposter is a game where everyone gets the same secret word. Except the imposter. They have no idea and have to fake it. Everyone takes turns giving a hint. Unmask the bluffer before they work out the word.

You might know it as the imposter game or who is the imposter. You play it with one phone that you pass around. Below you will learn how it works and how to turn it into a drinking game.

Setting up

You play Imposter in the AnyTimerApp. One phone is enough.

  1. Pick the number of players. You can play with up to 16.
  2. Pick the number of imposters. The more players, the more imposters you can have.
  3. Choose whether the imposter gets a hint. A hint makes bluffing easier.
  4. Pick one or more categories for the word.
  5. Sit in a circle.

With 3 to 7 players, 1 imposter is enough. From 8 players it gets more fun with 2.

How to play

  1. Pass the phone around. Everyone secretly checks their card and passes the phone on.
  2. Your card shows the secret word. Are you the imposter? Then it says so, maybe with a hint.
  3. Everyone takes turns giving one word as a hint. The hint fits the secret word, but doesn't name it.
  4. The imposter gives a hint too. They have to guess what fits the word.
  5. After one or two rounds you talk it over. Who gave a strange hint?
  6. Count to three and everyone points at who they think the imposter is.
  7. The player with the most votes shows their card.

An example

The secret word is pizza. The first player says "oven". The second says "Italy". The imposter doesn't know the word and says "warm". That just about fits. The next player says "cheese". After the round the group thinks "warm" was pretty vague. They point at the imposter.

Who wins?

  • The group finds the imposter: the imposter gets one guess at the word. Right? Then they still win. Wrong? Then the group wins.
  • The group picks the wrong player: the imposter wins.
  • More imposters: find them one by one. The group wins once every imposter is found.

Tips for good hints

  • Don't give a hint that is too obvious. Then the imposter knows the word straight away.
  • Don't give a hint that is too vague either. Then everyone thinks you are the imposter.
  • Are you the imposter? Listen closely to the hints before yours. They tell you a lot about the word.
  • Look at your card as long as everyone else. Passing the phone on too fast stands out.

Imposter as a drinking game

Imposter is already a party game. Add drinks and bluffing gets even more exciting. Pick one or more of these options. Agree on them before you start.

The imposter gets caught

Is the imposter found? Then they drink. Do they still guess the word? Then everyone else drinks.

The imposter wins

Does the imposter stay hidden? Then everyone drinks except them. Whoever pointed at the wrong player takes an extra sip.

A chug for the imposter

Is the imposter caught in the very first round? Then they down their drink in one go: an adtje.

With an anytimer at stake

An imposter who wins gets an anytimer from the player who was furthest off.
