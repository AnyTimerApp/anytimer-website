How to play

The rules of never have I ever are simple. All you need are your hands.

  1. Sit in a circle. Everyone holds up ten fingers.
  2. The youngest player starts. They say a sentence that starts with "Never have I ever". For example: "Never have I ever passed an exam without opening the book."
  3. Have you done it? Then put a finger down.
  4. Then it's the next player's turn on the left. Keep going round.
  5. The first person with no fingers left is out. The last one with fingers up wins.

Other ways to play

  • As a drinking game you take a sip instead of putting a finger down. Nobody is out, so you play as long as you like. If you keep the fingers too, the first person with none left downs their drink.
  • In the I have version you flip it round. You name something you have done, and anyone who never has puts a finger down.
  • For the pot version everyone writes three questions on slips of paper first. They go in a cup and you take turns drawing one, without knowing who wrote it.
  • With two teams you add up the fingers per team. The team with the most fingers left wins.

Writing your own question? Keep this in mind

  • Pick one specific moment. After "Never have I ever lost my phone on a night out" someone has to tell how it happened. After "Never have I ever done something stupid" not much happens.
  • Aim for something about half the group has done. If everyone or nobody puts a finger down, there is nothing to tell.
  • Save the 18+ questions for later in the night.
  • If you're not sure a question is OK, skip it.
