30 Seconds

30 Seconds is a team game where you get your team to guess as many words as possible in 30 seconds. As a drinking game every failed round turns into a small penalty for your team.

How to play

  1. Split the group into teams of at least two players.
  2. One player takes a card and describes the words on it.
  3. The team tries to guess as many words as possible within 30 seconds.
  4. You can't say the word itself. Translating or spelling it doesn't count either.
  5. After the round, count how many words were guessed right.
  6. Then it's the next team's turn.

When do you drink?

  • For every word that wasn't guessed, the team takes a sip.
  • Does the player explaining say the word by accident? Then they drink.
  • The team with the lowest score downs a drink.

Extra rules

  • Play to a fixed score. The first team to reach 20 right answers wins.
  • Want to play faster? Skip the board and only use the cards.
  • Only play hotseat rounds: 30 seconds in which your team has to guess three words. Didn't make it? Then you down a drink.
