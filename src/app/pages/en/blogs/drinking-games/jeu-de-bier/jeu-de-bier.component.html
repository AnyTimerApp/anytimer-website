Jeu de bier

Jeu de bier is a Dutch drinking game. Think pétanque, but on a table and with beer caps. The bar is set a little lower too. Perfect to kick off the night.

You need almost nothing. A few caps. A target. A table. And people who think they can aim really well.

What you need
Beer caps A target or pen

In Jeu de bier you slide or throw beer caps towards a target on the table or on the floor. The target can be another beer cap or something you draw with a pen. Think of a dot, a circle or a cross.

Every player gets a few caps. Then everyone takes turns sliding or throwing a cap towards the target. Whoever gets closest wins the round.

How does it work?

First draw a target on the table. Use something that is easy to wipe off. A piece of paper with a cross on it works fine too.

Give every player three beer caps.

Then you play like this:

  1. Everyone takes turns sliding or throwing one cap towards the target.
  2. Keep going until all caps have been played.
  3. Check which caps are closest to the target.
  4. The player with the best cap wins the round.
  5. Everyone else drinks.

When do you drink?

Different versions

  • The winner of the round or the game gets to hand out a drink to down
  • The winner gets to hand out a sip
  • The winner doesn't drink. The rest does. If the winner has 2 caps closer than number 2, the rest drinks 2 sips. 3 caps means 3 sips, and so on.

Extra rules for more chaos

Jeu de bier doesn't need many rules. But a few extra rules do make it more fun.

  • Does your cap land right on the target? Hand out sips
  • Does your cap fall off the table? Drink.
  • Win two rounds in a row? Double sips.
