Fuck the Dealer

Fuck the Dealer is a card game where everyone tries to beat the dealer. You guess the value of the card and the dealer has to give hints when you are wrong.

How to play

  1. Take the jokers out of the deck and shuffle the cards.
  2. Pick a dealer. Do it at random or with the highest card.
  3. The dealer looks at the top card.
  4. The player to the left of the dealer guesses the value of the card.
  5. Guessed it right away? Then the dealer drinks 2 sips and it's the next player's turn.
  6. Is the first guess wrong? Then the dealer says whether the card is higher or lower. The player gets one more guess.
  7. Right on the second guess? Then the dealer drinks 1 sip.
  8. Is the second guess wrong too? Then the player drinks 1 sip.
  9. Then put the card face up on the table, in order. That way you can see which cards have been played.
  10. Once the dealer has won three rounds in a row, the dealer can pass the deck clockwise.
  11. The next person is now the dealer.

When do you drink?

  • The dealer who is stuck with the deck at the end downs a drink.

Extra rules

  • Put played cards face down on a pile.
  • Agree beforehand whether the ace is high or low.
