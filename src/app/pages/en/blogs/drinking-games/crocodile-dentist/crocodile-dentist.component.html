Crocodile Dentist drinking game

The Crocodile Dentist drinking game is the classic kids' game with a drinking rule. Everyone takes turns pressing a tooth and hopes the jaw stays open. In the Netherlands the game is called Krokodil met Kiespijn.

How to play

  1. Put the crocodile on the table with its mouth open.
  2. Agree on the penalty for when the jaw snaps shut.
  3. Decide who starts.
  4. The first player presses 1 tooth.
  5. Does the jaw stay open? Then it's the next player's turn.
  6. Does the jaw snap shut? Then that player loses the round.
  7. The loser takes the penalty you agreed on.
  8. Open the mouth again.
  9. The loser starts the next round.

When do you drink?

  • You drink when the jaw snaps shut on your tooth.
  • The loser gets to pick the drink for the next round.

Extra rules

  • Don't let anyone feel the teeth before pressing.
  • Agree on a time limit to think. For example 5 seconds.
