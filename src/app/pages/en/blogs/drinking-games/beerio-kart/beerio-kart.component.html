Beerio Kart (Mario Barf)

Beerio Kart is Mario Kart with a drink next to your controller. Your drink has to be empty before you cross the finish line. But drinking while you drive is not allowed. In the Netherlands this game is called Mario Barf.

How to play

  1. Every player puts a drink next to their controller.
  2. Start a normal race in Mario Kart.
  3. Everyone tries to race and finish their drink at the same time.
  4. You can only drink when your kart is standing completely still.
  5. You can only finish once your drink is empty.
  6. Crossed the finish line with drink left? Then you count as last.

When do you drink?

  • You drink during the race, at moments when you stand still.
  • The last player downs a drink or gets the penalty you agreed on.
  • Forgot to drink and finished anyway? You still finish your glass and get a penalty.

Extra rules

  • Play with the rule that your controller has to be on the table while you drink.
  • You can also agree that the winner gets to hand out a drink to down.
  • In teams, count the worst place of the team. Or only the best place.
