Rage Cage (Stress Pong)

Rage Cage is a fast take on beer pong with two cups going round at the same time. As soon as someone catches up with you, you drink. And the pressure only gets higher. It's also called Stack Cup. In the Netherlands it's called Stress Pong.

How to play

  1. Put a bunch of filled cups in the middle.
  2. Give two players sitting opposite each other an empty cup and a ping pong ball.
  3. Both players try to bounce the ball off the table into their own cup.
  4. Made it in one go? Then you can give the cup to any player you like.
  5. Didn't make it in one go? Then pass the cup to your left as soon as you score.
  6. Does the cup behind you catch up and get stacked into yours? Then you're caught.
  7. Grab a cup from the middle, drink it and keep playing with that cup.

When do you drink?

  • You drink when your cup gets caught and stacked.
  • Hit a cup in the middle by accident? Then you drink that cup.
  • The last player with a cup drinks the last cup.

Extra rules

  • Don't fill the cups too much. The game is fast enough as it is.
  • Agree whether slapping cups away is allowed. In Rage Cage stacking is often calmer than smashing.
