Chug Machine rules

Chug Machine is a slot machine for chugs. You spin three reels and each reel stops on a full or an empty mug. Three full mugs? Then you get to hand out a chug. Three empty mugs? Then you have to down your drink yourself.

You play it in the AnyTimerApp. So all you need is your phone and drinks. In the Dutch version of the app the game is called Bakmachine. Below you will learn how it works.

How to play

  1. Open Chug Machine in the AnyTimerApp.
  2. Sit in a circle. The phone goes round, one player at a time.
  3. The player whose turn it is spins the three reels.
  4. Each reel stops on a full or an empty mug. Both are equally likely on every reel.
  5. Check the result and do what goes with it.
  6. Pass the phone to the next player.

When someone has to drink

  • Three full mugs: you hand out a chug. Pick who has to down their drink.
  • Three empty mugs: you have to down your drink yourself. In Dutch that is called bak trekken.
  • Three full mugs twice in a row: you get to hand out even more. How much is up to you. Agree on it before you start.
  • Any other result: nothing happens. Next player.

The odds

Every reel has a 50% chance of a full mug. So the chance of three full mugs is 1 in 8. Same for three empty mugs. On average someone downs a drink every four turns.

Extra rules

  • Agree beforehand what a chug is. A full glass, half a glass or a few sips.
  • Playing with a big group? Then three full mugs means you hand out a chug to two players.
  • Whoever has to down their drink doesn't spin first again. The next player goes.
