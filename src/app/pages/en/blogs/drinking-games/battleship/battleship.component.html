Battleship drinking game

The Battleship drinking game is the classic board game with one drinking rule added. Hit the other player's ship and they have to drink. In Dutch the game is called Zeeslag.

How to play

  1. Set up the Battleship board.
  2. Both players place their ships on their own grid.
  3. Make sure the other player can't see where your ships are.
  4. The first player calls out a coordinate. For example B3.
  5. The other player checks if there is a ship there.
  6. Is it a hit? Then the player says it's a hit.
  7. Is it a miss? Then the player says it's a miss.
  8. After a miss, it's the other player's turn.
  9. After a hit you can agree that the same player gets another shot.
  10. Keep track of which squares you have already called.
  11. The game stops as soon as all ships of one player have sunk.

When do you drink?

  • You drink when your ship gets hit.
  • You drink extra when a ship sinks.
  • The loser takes the final penalty you agreed on.

Extra rules

  • Down a drink when a ship sinks.
  • No board? Draw two grids with letters and numbers on paper.
