Shut the Box rules

Shut the Box is a dice game with a wooden box and the numbers 1 to 9. You roll two dice and flip down numbers that add up to your roll. Manage to flip down every number? Then you have shut the box and you win straight away.

You will get the rules in two minutes. Below you will learn step by step how it works. At the bottom you will find how to turn it into a drinking game.

Setting up

A Shut the Box is a box with a row of flaps. Each flap shows a number from 1 to 9. You roll the dice inside the box.

  1. Open all the flaps, so you can see every number.
  2. Get two dice ready.
  3. Grab a pen and paper to keep score.
  4. Agree on how many rounds you play. Usually you play until someone has 45 points.

No Shut the Box at home? Write the numbers 1 to 9 on a piece of paper. Cross out every number you flip down. Then all you need is two dice.

How to play

  1. The first player rolls two dice and adds up the total.
  2. Flip down numbers that add up to that total. That can be one number or several.
  3. Roll again and flip down more numbers. You can only use numbers that are still open.
  4. Are the 7, 8 and 9 down? Then you may choose to roll just one die.
  5. Can't flip down any numbers with your roll? Then your turn is over.
  6. Add up the numbers that are still open. That is your score for this round.
  7. Open all the flaps again. Now it is the next player's turn.

An example

You roll a 5 and a 3. That makes 8. You can now flip down the 8. Or the 7 and the 1, the 6 and the 2, the 5 and the 3, or the 5, the 2 and the 1. Your choice.

Tip: flip down the high numbers as early as you can. Low numbers are easy to combine at the end. A 9 only goes down with a high roll.

Who wins?

After each round you write down everyone's score. The lowest score wins the round.

Does someone flip down every number? Then they have shut the box. They score 0 and win the round straight away. Some groups even give them the whole game.

Playing several rounds? Then add up the scores. Reach 45 points or more and you are out. The last player left wins.

House rules

Shut the Box is played in lots of ways. These are the differences you will see most. Agree on them before you start.

  • Scoring: most groups add up the open numbers. Some read them as one number. Are the 1 and the 4 still open? Then you score 14 instead of 5.
  • One die: some groups allow it once the 7, 8 and 9 are down. Others only when less than 7 is left open in total.
  • One round or a full game: play one round for a quick winner, or up to 45 points for a longer game.

Shut the Box with 12 numbers

Some boxes have the numbers 1 to 12. The rules stay the same. The game just takes longer and shutting the box is much harder. With 12 numbers you usually play up to 78 points instead of 45.

There are also boxes for 4 or 6 players. Each player has their own row of flaps. You then play at the same time instead of taking turns.

Shut the Box as a drinking game

On its own Shut the Box is a calm game. Add drinks and every roll gets exciting. Pick one or more of these options. Agree on them before you start.

Highest score drinks

Whoever has the most points after a round takes a sip for every 10 points. Got 23 points? Then you take 2 sips.

Lowest score hands out sips

Whoever has the fewest points hands out sips. They can split them over the other players.

Box shut

Does someone shut the box? Then everyone else downs their drink in one go: an adtje.

With an anytimer at stake

The winner of the game gets an anytimer from the player with the most points.
