Trek je Bak Mania

Trek je Bak Mania is the Dutch Trackmania drinking game with one simple penalty. Everyone takes turns driving and the worst driver of the round downs a drink. The name is a Dutch joke: "trek je bak" means "down your drink".

How to play

  1. Pick a track in Trackmania (auto generated ones are best).
  2. Turn on hotseat mode if it's available.
  3. Every player takes turns driving the same track.
  4. Play until the fuel runs out or agree how many tries everyone gets.
  5. Write down the times or let the game keep the score.
  6. After the round the slowest player downs a drink.

When do you drink?

  • The player with the worst time downs a drink.
  • Didn't reach the finish? Drink extra.

Extra rules

  • Let the winner pick the next track.
  • The winner gets to hand out a drink to down.
  • Play short tracks. That keeps the pace high.
