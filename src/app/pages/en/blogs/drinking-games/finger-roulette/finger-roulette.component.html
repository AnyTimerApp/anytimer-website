Finger Roulette

Finger Roulette is a game you play on your phone. Everyone puts a finger on the screen and the phone picks one.

How to play

  1. First agree on what the chosen person has to do.
  2. Open Finger Roulette in the AnyTimerApp.
  3. Everyone puts a finger on the phone screen.
  4. Keep your finger still on the screen.
  5. The phone picks 1 of the fingers.
  6. The owner of that finger does what the group agreed on.

Did you know you can play Finger Roulette in our free app?

What does the chosen person have to do?

The group decides that before everyone puts a finger on the screen. The chosen person:

  • has lost;
  • has won;
  • or has to down their drink.

A round only takes 10 seconds. So you can quickly play a few rounds in a row.
