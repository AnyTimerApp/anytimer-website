Drinking games without cards: 10 games for any night

No cards at home? No problem. For these drinking games you need dice, cups, a glass or just your phone. You can even play Vikings with nothing but drinks.

For every game you see how many people can play and what you need. The full rules are always one click away.

01Mexico

  • Players: 2 to 8
  • What you need: 2 dice, Drinks

Mexico is a dice game with two dice. Don't throw the lowest roll of the round and hope for a Mexico. The Dutch call it Mexen.

Read the full Mexico rules.

02Rage Cage

  • Players: 4 to 12
  • What you need: Plastic cups, 2 ping pong balls, Drinks

Rage Cage is a fast beer pong game with two cups going round at once. Get stacked? Then you drink. In the Netherlands it's called Stress Pong.

Read the full Rage Cage rules.

03Jeu de bier

  • Players: 2 to 8
  • What you need: Beer caps, A target or pen

Jeu de bier is a Dutch table game with beer caps. Think pétanque, but on a table. A simple skill game to kick off the night.

Read the full Jeu de bier rules.

04Fingers

  • Players: 4 to 8
  • What you need: Glass, Drinks

Fingers is a quick guessing game with fingers on a glass. Guess how many fingers stay on the rim and get out of the game.

Read the full Fingers rules.

05Vikings

  • Players: 3 to 16
  • What you need: Drinks

Vikings is a game where everyone has to pay attention at once. One player is the Viking, the neighbours row and the rest are the sea.

Read the full Vikings rules.

0630 Seconds

  • Players: 4 to 16
  • What you need: 30 Seconds, Timer, Drinks

Guess as many words as you can in 30 seconds. As a drinking game every failed round means a small penalty for your team.

Read the full 30 Seconds rules.

07Crocodile Dentist drinking game

  • Players: 4 to 8
  • What you need: Crocodile Dentist, Drinks

The Crocodile Dentist drinking game is the kids' game with a twist. Everyone presses a tooth and hopes the jaw stays open.

Read the full Crocodile Dentist drinking game rules.

08Looping Louie

  • Players: 2 to 4
  • What you need: Looping Louie, Drinks

Looping Louie is a skill game with a little plane that flies in circles. Protect your chickens and send Louie to everyone else's.

Read the full Looping Louie rules.

09Beerio Kart

  • Players: 2 to 4
  • What you need: Mario Kart, Console, Controllers, Drinks

Beerio Kart is Mario Kart with a drink next to your controller. Your drink has to be empty before you cross the finish line.

Read the full Beerio Kart rules.

10Angry Mug

  • Players: 2+
  • What you need: Phone

Angry Mug is an exciting game of luck on your phone. Tap the happy mugs, but watch out for the angry one.

Read the full Angry Mug rules.
