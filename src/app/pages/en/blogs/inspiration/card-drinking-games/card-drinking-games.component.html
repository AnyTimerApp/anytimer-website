Card drinking games: the 8 best games with cards

There is a deck of cards in almost every student house. That is all you need for a good night.

These are the 8 best card drinking games. For every game you see how many people can play and what you need. The full rules are always one click away.

01Kingsen

  • Players: 3 to 12
  • What you need: Playing cards, Shot glass, Drinks

Kingsen is the Dutch version of Kings Cup. Every card comes with a task. Just try not to be the one who draws the last king.

Read the full Kingsen rules.

02Ride the Bus

  • Players: 2 to 8
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Ride the Bus is a card game with a few rounds. First you collect cards. Then the pyramid decides who has to ride the bus.

Read the full Ride the Bus rules.

03Fuck the Dealer

  • Players: 3 to 8
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Fuck the Dealer is a card game where everyone tries to beat the dealer by guessing the value of the next card.

Read the full Fuck the Dealer rules.

04Higher or Lower

  • Players: 2 to 10
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Higher or Lower is a simple card game where you keep guessing. Will the next card be higher or lower?

Read the full Higher or Lower rules.

05Ring of Fire

  • Players: 2 to 12
  • What you need: Playing cards, Cup, Drinks

Ring of Fire, also known as Kings Cup, is the classic card drinking game. Draw a card from the ring and do the task that goes with it.

Read the full Ring of Fire rules.

06Horse Race (Camel Race)

  • Players: 2 to 12
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Horse Race, also called Camel Race, is a card game where the four aces race each other. Bet your sips and hope your horse wins.

Read the full Horse Race (Camel Race) rules.

07Bakplaten

  • Players: 2 to 10
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Bakplaten is a super simple Dutch card game. Draw a face card? Then it's your turn to drink.

Read the full Bakplaten rules.

08Mijnenveld

  • Players: 1 to 10
  • What you need: Playing cards

Mijnenveld is a Dutch card drinking game. Pick a path through the cards. Step on a face card or an ace and you start over.

Read the full Mijnenveld rules.
