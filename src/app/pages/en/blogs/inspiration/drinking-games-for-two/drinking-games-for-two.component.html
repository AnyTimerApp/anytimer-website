Drinking games for 2 people: 10 games for two

Not every drinking game needs a whole group. You can play these 10 games with just the two of you. Perfect for a quiet night with your housemate.

For every game you see how many people can play and what you need. The full rules are always one click away.

01Battleship drinking game

  • Players: 2
  • What you need: Battleship, Drinks

The Battleship drinking game is the classic board game with one extra rule. Hit someone's ship and they have to drink.

Read the full Battleship drinking game rules.

02Mexico

  • Players: 2 to 8
  • What you need: 2 dice, Drinks

Mexico is a dice game with two dice. Don't throw the lowest roll of the round and hope for a Mexico. The Dutch call it Mexen.

Read the full Mexico rules.

03Higher or Lower

  • Players: 2 to 10
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Higher or Lower is a simple card game where you keep guessing. Will the next card be higher or lower?

Read the full Higher or Lower rules.

04Bakplaten

  • Players: 2 to 10
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Bakplaten is a super simple Dutch card game. Draw a face card? Then it's your turn to drink.

Read the full Bakplaten rules.

05Jeu de bier

  • Players: 2 to 8
  • What you need: Beer caps, A target or pen

Jeu de bier is a Dutch table game with beer caps. Think pétanque, but on a table. A simple skill game to kick off the night.

Read the full Jeu de bier rules.

06Beerio Kart

  • Players: 2 to 4
  • What you need: Mario Kart, Console, Controllers, Drinks

Beerio Kart is Mario Kart with a drink next to your controller. Your drink has to be empty before you cross the finish line.

Read the full Beerio Kart rules.

07Looping Louie

  • Players: 2 to 4
  • What you need: Looping Louie, Drinks

Looping Louie is a skill game with a little plane that flies in circles. Protect your chickens and send Louie to everyone else's.

Read the full Looping Louie rules.

08Horse Race (Camel Race)

  • Players: 2 to 12
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Horse Race, also called Camel Race, is a card game where the four aces race each other. Bet your sips and hope your horse wins.

Read the full Horse Race (Camel Race) rules.

09Ride the Bus

  • Players: 2 to 8
  • What you need: Playing cards, Drinks

Ride the Bus is a card game with a few rounds. First you collect cards. Then the pyramid decides who has to ride the bus.

Read the full Ride the Bus rules.

10Angry Mug

  • Players: 2+
  • What you need: Phone

Angry Mug is an exciting game of luck on your phone. Tap the happy mugs, but watch out for the angry one.

Read the full Angry Mug rules.
