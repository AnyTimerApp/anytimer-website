Can you refuse an anytimer?

Short answer: yes, you can. But you usually don't get away with it that easily. You can use an anytimer whenever you want. So also when you are hungover or when it suits you the least. It is an anytimer, not a convenience-timer or a whenever-I-feel-like-it-timer.

Of course you can

Technically: of course. Nobody is going to force you. But within a group of friends there is usually a price to pay.

What happens when you refuse an anytimer?

Your group of friends makes its own rules for this. Either way, you earn a penalty. The gold standard is doubling the any that was used. That is the most logical rule. Otherwise you could just refuse every time.

When can't you use an anytimer?

Your group decides this together too. Some examples:

  • Before driving
  • Before a funeral
  • Before an exam
  • When someone is sick

Basically you assume your friends have a certain amount of common sense, so nobody gets put in danger.
