Privacy policy

Version 2.1  ·  Last updated: 15 September 2026

At Anytimer we handle your data with care. This policy explains which data we collect, why we collect it and how you stay in control.

This is a translation of our Dutch privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Dutch version prevails.

01General

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Anytimerapp ("the App") and to the website anytimer.app ("the Website"). Anytimerapp processes personal data in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG) and other applicable privacy laws.

02Data controller

The controller responsible for processing personal data is:

Anytimer The Netherlands info@anytimer.app

03What data we collect

When you use the App, we may process the following personal data:

  • Account data: email address, username and profile picture.
  • User content: content created by users, such as images and videos, including reported content.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, operating system, app version and device IDs.
  • Usage data: information about how you use the App, such as app interactions and feature usage.

04How we collect data

We collect data in the following ways:

  • Directly from you: data you provide when you create an account, post content or submit a report.
  • Automatically: technical and usage data that is collected automatically when you use the App.
  • Through external services: data we receive from third party login services (Google, Apple) when you choose to log in with them.

05Purpose of processing

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Making it possible to create and manage an account.
  • Making sure the App works properly.
  • Offering social features and the feed.
  • Understanding how the App is used, for analysis and improvement.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Moderating content and enforcing our Terms of use, including reviewing reports of inappropriate content.
  • Communicating with users when needed.

Data collected for a specific purpose is not used for other purposes without your additional consent.

06Legal basis

We process personal data based on:

  • Performance of the agreement: processing that is needed to deliver the service (account management, app features).
  • Legitimate interest: improving and securing the App, preventing fraud and moderating content.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, we ask for your explicit consent.

07Sharing data with third parties

We only share personal data with third parties when this is needed for the App to work. We use the following types of service providers:

  • Hosting providers: to store and process data (cloud infrastructure).
  • Authentication services: Google and Apple for logging in.
  • Analytics services: to understand app usage and performance.

All third parties are contractually required to protect personal data at the same level as described in this policy. Personal data is never sold to third parties and is not shared with third party AI systems.

08Tracking & Advertising

The App does not track you for advertising and does not use third party ad networks. We do not use any tracking that requires an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) request.

09Cookies on the Website

On the Website we use cookies from the following parties:

  • Google Analytics: to measure visitor numbers and understand how the Website is used.
  • Microsoft Clarity: to analyse click behaviour and make the Website easier to use.

These cookies are only placed after you give permission through the cookie notice on the Website. If you decline, these cookies are not placed. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the cookies for anytimer.app in your browser.

10Retention

Personal data is not kept longer than needed for the purposes it was collected for:

  • Account data: kept as long as your account is active and deleted after you delete your account.
  • User content: kept as long as your account is active or until you delete the content yourself.
  • Technical and usage data: kept for a limited period for analysis and security.

Data may be kept longer if the law requires it.

11Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all related data at any time through the settings in the App. After deletion your personal data is removed immediately and permanently, unless the law requires us to keep it.

12Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure and alteration.

13Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: you can ask which data we process about you.
  • Right to rectification: you can have incorrect data corrected.
  • Right to erasure: you can ask us to delete your data.
  • Right to object: you can object to the processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability: you can ask to receive your data in a structured format.
  • Right to withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent you gave earlier at any time.

You can use these rights by contacting us at info@anytimer.app. We respond to your request within 30 days.

If you believe your personal data is processed unlawfully, you have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

14Children & Age

The App is only meant for users who are 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from people under 18. If we find out that we collected data from a minor, it will be deleted immediately. If you suspect that a minor has given us their data, please contact us at info@anytimer.app.

15Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If there are important changes, we will let you know through the App. The latest version is always available in the App and at anytimer.app/en/privacy.

16Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal data, you can contact:

Anytimer The Netherlands info@anytimer.app
