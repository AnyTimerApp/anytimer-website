Beer chug timer

Time your chug for free with this chugging timer. It works right in your browser, on your phone or laptop. Tap and release the blue screen to start. Tap again when your beer or other drink is gone.

It's the same Any timer as in the AnyTimerApp. You see your time down to a hundredth of a second.

How the chug timer works

  1. Tap the blue screen and keep your finger on it. The timer jumps to zero.
  2. Let go. From that moment the clock is running.
  3. Chug. Finish your beer in one go.
  4. Tap the screen. The timer stops and you see your time.

The timer only starts when you let go. So you know exactly when the time begins.

On a laptop the space bar works too. Press it, let go and press again.

Why the 3 second wait after every time?

After stopping, the timer locks for 3 seconds. That's on purpose. This way you can't mistap or double tap by accident and lose your time.

So you can hit the screen anywhere you like. A double tap won't ruin your time. You focus on your chug, not on the timer.

What is a drinking timer?

A drinking timer, or beer timer, measures how fast you finish a drink. Usually that's a chug: a glass of beer in one go. The clock runs from the moment you let go until you tap again. So you see exactly who's fastest.

You use a drinking timer for things like:

  • A chugging contest against your housemates.
  • A boat race, the team relay where everyone chugs in turn. Time the whole team and try to beat your record.
  • A lost bet or an anytimer someone just used.
  • Drinking games where speed counts, like Beerio Kart.

How to chug a beer faster

Want a better time on the chug timer? These tips help.

  • Pick a drink that isn't too fizzy. Bubbles slow you down.
  • Take a breath before you let go of the screen.
  • Tilt your head and the glass back at the same time.
  • Don't take small gulps. Open your throat and let it flow.

And know your limit. A fast time isn't worth a bad night.

Why the Dutch call it an adt timer

In the Netherlands a chug is called an adtje. Everyone spells it differently. Adtje, atje or adje. So Dutch students search for an adt timer, ad timer or at timer. It's all the same thing: a timer for your chug.
