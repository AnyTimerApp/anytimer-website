Een drankspel is een spelletje waarbij drinken onderdeel is van de regels. Verlies je een ronde, maak je een fout of ben je te laat? Dan neem je een slok, trek je een bak of krijg je een andere soort straf. Sommige drankspellen speel je met kaarten, andere met dobbelstenen.

Wat is studententaal?

Studententaal is de verzamelnaam voor alle woorden, afkortingen en uitdrukkingen die binnen het studentenleven ontstaan. Van drinktermen als adten en bak trekken tot verenigingsjargon als dispuut en KMT: elke studentenstad en vereniging heeft zijn eigen woordenschat, maar veel termen worden overal herkend.

What is a drinking game?

A drinking game is a game where drinking is part of the rules. Lose a round, make a mistake or react too late? Then you take a sip, down your drink or get another penalty. Some drinking games use cards. Others use dice, a phone or nothing at all.

International games and Dutch classics

You will find the games everyone knows, like Ring of Fire, Ride the Bus and Rage Cage. But also the Dutch classics that internationals bump into at their first student party, like Kingsen, Bakplaten and Mijnenveld. Every game here is free to play. Most of the time you only need what is already lying around: a deck of cards, a few dice, a bottle cap or a pen.

Use the filter above to pick card games, dice games, skill games or other drinking games. Not sure where to start? Check out our top 10 favourite drinking games for a quick start.

What is Dutch student slang?

Dutch students have their own language. It is full of short words, abbreviations and traditions you will not find in any textbook. Some words come from drinking culture, like adten and bak trekken. Others come from student associations, like dispuut and KMT. Every city has its own words, but many of them are used all over the country. Dutch words get a "Dutch student slang" label, so you always know which ones you will only hear here.

The words you will hear in your first week

Feeling brak on a Sunday. Indrinken before going out. The difference between your studentenhuis and thuisthuis. And of course the anytimer: a free pass between friends that you can use whenever you want. Read what an anytimer is and how it works, or find out what an adtje is.
